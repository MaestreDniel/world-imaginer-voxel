## Goal

From the surface the player finds, now and then, a hole or a ravine that leads down. Underground there are several kinds of cave, each with its own look: wide interconnected "cheese" caverns with stone pillars, long winding spaghetti tunnels (3D and a flatter 2D layer), thin noodle tubes, worm tunnels with flat floors, ravines, and occasionally a very large interior space. Where caves meet a chunk border, a depth limit or another kind of cave, no flat wall or clean cut shows; neighbouring regions mix the kinds differently so no rhythm repeats. Caves stay below lakes, rivers and seas until SP7 brings aquifers. Worlds saved before SP6a open with the caves too (D36). Every size, frequency, depth range and rarity is a parameter on the map's panel, in a new Caves section, regenerating the world around the player as SP4c does. X shows the X-ray: rock, dirt and water vanish and the caves' hollows remain, translucent and coloured by cave type, within a depth band moved with PageUp and PageDown, the surface a faint film. Light level 0 is no longer pure black but a faint floor; L (or a Settings checkbox) turns on night vision. Underneath: the cave families are data in the default density tree, one `interpolated` slot per family; carvers are pure functions of their source chunk, cached, carved in T before water and surface rules; T writes a cave-type tag per carved voxel into a new store channel, which the X-ray, the slice tint and the metrics read. Measured by C1-C6 (master), new C7 (cuts), C8 (variety) and C9 (entrances and the surface), L2, S1, DT1, DT2, P1 (T ≤ 13 ms p50 and ≤ 20 ms p99, `terrain.islands` ≤ 14.5 / 26.5 ms: ruling R14), the DAG-closure kill criterion and two Chrome bench legs. The generated world changes: `GENERATOR_VERSION` 5 → 6 at the first push that changes generated output and 6 → 7 at the second, and the goldens are re-recorded (D28).

## Decisions taken by this spec (for the user's review)

### Decisions with a visible effect

- **D1 The cave kinds** (§1.2, §2). *You notice:* eight kinds underground. **Cheese**: large rounded caverns joined into networks, with thin stone pillars standing in them, never within about 12 blocks of the surface. **Spaghetti 3D**: long tunnels a few blocks wide winding in every direction. **Spaghetti 2D**: flatter, wider tunnels that follow a wavy layer around y −64 … 64. **Noodles**: thin tubes 1-3 blocks wide below y 64. **Entrances**: tunnels that reach the surface and open there. **Worms**: carved tunnels with flat floors, tapered ends and an occasional fork or small room at the start. **Ravines**: tall narrow cuts with rough walls, often open to the sky. **Underground worms**: like worms, starting below y 24 and narrowing to nothing between y 24 and y 40, so none reaches above y 40 and none ends on a flat ceiling (the cut line, §15). The master's formulas are kept except where D2 changes them. Alternative: the master's formulas verbatim (§1.3 lists what that keeps).
- **D2 No abrupt cuts** (§1.3, §1.4). *You notice:* no tunnel ends in a flat wall where a family's depth limit, rarity band or on/off switch lies; noodles close smoothly; caves fade out over 8 blocks below the surface instead of ending on a plane; at a depth limit, under water and under the floating islands' cap, caves narrow over about 16 blocks before they close. The rule: every cave term is continuous in x, y and z, a hard switch is allowed only where it cannot change the voxel's sign, and every closing cap has a gentle part (the family's value rises by about its own typical air depth over 16 blocks, so the cave shrinks gradually) before a steep part that guarantees closure. Continuity alone is not enough: a cap 20 times steeper than the family closes it within a block of a plane. The master's formulas break the rule in five places (§1.3), each replaced. Alternative: keep the master's switches and only measure the walls they leave (C7).
- **D3 Occasional large interiors; neighbouring caves differ** (§1.2). *You notice:* in some regions, about one in five 512-block areas, cheese caverns grow several times larger (the user's "espacios extensos"); in others, noodles and spaghetti dominate. Two regional 2D noises (λ 1536 and 1024) shift the cheese threshold and the noodle/spaghetti balance. Alternative: no regional noise (caverns of one size everywhere, rhythm judged by C8 only).
- **D4 Entrances: occasional, the surface stays terrain** (§1.2, §1.4). *You notice:* entrance tunnels and ravines open to the sky now and then (this spec's proposal, shown to the user with the measurements at tuning: within about 192 blocks of most places, at most about 150 openings per km², at most 2 % of land positions holed); deep caves never break the surface. Alternative: the master's switch at 8 blocks (a plane where every deep family stops).
- **D5 Worms and ravines** (§2). *You notice:* worms of 85-112 steps, about 2-12 blocks across (estimate from §2.2's radii), flat-floored, ending round; one in four forks at its middle; one in four starts with a small room (D35); underground worms the same below y 40; ravines 84-112 blocks long, up to about 15 wide and 45 tall, rough-walled, narrowing at both ends. Under water and under the islands' cap carvers narrow to nothing instead of stopping on a plane. No carver ever stops at a chunk border or at the edge of its generation window. Alternative: the master's 17 × 17 window, which truncates the longest worms (§2.1).
- **D6 Grass and cave floors** (§3.1). *You notice:* a ravine floor open to the sky gets its biome's top (grass, sand, snow), cave floors and walls are stone or deepslate (cave-biome floors arrive in SP6b), and grass never grows under a cave ceiling. Carving runs in T before water v0 and the surface pass, so the pass skins the carved column. Alternative: carve after the surface pass with the master's "carved dirt below grass becomes grass" rule.
- **D7 Caves and water until SP7** (§1.4, §2.4). *You notice:* under a lake, river or sea, caves stay at least about 16 blocks below the bed, measured from the terrain's own surface there (not from the column's mean height, which can lie 9 to more than 30 blocks above a coastal bed), and narrow over the 16 blocks below that (no water wall inside a cave, no flooded shaft next to a dry cave). One continuous "wet roof" replaces the master's `lakeRoof` and covers rivers and seas too, and it reaches full strength at every position water v0 fills. SP7's aquifers decide whether caves may open under water. Alternative: the master's lake roof only, with vertical water walls in coastal caves and under rivers until SP7.
- **D8 Floating islands** (§1.4, §2.4). *You notice:* in the floating_islands preset, caves narrow from y 156, are closed by y 176 and never reach y 180, so no hollow mountain top is ever taken for an island (SP3e's hand-over). ### Technical decisions (no visible effect)

- **D17 One `interpolated` slot per cave family** (§1.1). Each family (entrances, cheese, spaghetti 3D, spaghetti 2D, pillars, and the noodle's four noises) is its own `tap(name, interpolated(…))`; the root combines them per voxel. It gives sharper intersections (Minecraft's form), makes the cave-type tag exact and cheap (a choice among values already computed, read through a compiled tap reader, §3.2), and lets the inspector mute one family at a time (master :678). Cost estimate: about 9 trilerps more per voxel in straddling cells, +0.3-0.6 ms per column by the first estimate, up to about 3-4.5 ms by the technical review's count (§1.6); D25 measures it. Alternative: the master's single `interpolated` around the whole cave value (cheaper per voxel, but the tag must then be recomputed per family in a separate job, approximately).
- **D18 One new Expr op, `noiseScaled{id, s}`** (§1.5): a density noise sampled at `(x/s, y/s, z/s)`, for the spaghetti rarity scales. Every other term is written with existing ops (`cube(x)` = `mul(square(x), x)`, `yGrad` = `slide(const 1, knots)`, toggles = `rangeChoice`). A new op rather than a field on `noise`, so `exprHash` of existing trees and the `sp3b.*` goldens do not move. Alternative: separate noise leaves per scale (no new op; about 48 more panel leaves, and the scales are no longer the same noise stretched).
- **D19 No level-field −∞ rule** (§1.4). ## 1. The density tree with caves

### 1.1 Shape of the default tree (D17)

Notation as master §3.6: values in blocks (×16 where the master writes `16·family`); `T` is the terrain tap; `body` the current terrain body (`DEFAULT_DENSITY_TREE`, `src/core/params/defaultTrees.ts:32-112`), unchanged. `rangeChoice(x, lo, hi, a, b)` is `a` where lo ≤ x < hi, else `b`.

```
T        = tap terrain( interpolated( ref body ) )                                    unchanged
detailG  = rangeChoice(interpolated(ref body), −1e9, 16, detail, 0)                   detail unchanged (VOXEL); skipped where T ≥ 16
fl(v)    = max(v, −48)                                                                 the family floor F = 48 (tree; §1.4)
E        = tap entrances( interpolated( fl(16·entr) + cap_e ) )
C        = tap cheese(    interpolated( sky( fl(16·cheese) + ramp + cap_c ) ) )
S3       = tap spag3d(    interpolated( sky( fl(16·spag3D) + ramp + cap_s3 ) ) )
S2       = tap spag2d(    interpolated( sky( fl(16·spag2D) + ramp + cap_s2 ) ) )
P        = tap pillars(   interpolated( 16·pillars ) )
keep     = rangeChoice(P·(1/16), −1e9, threshold, −1e6, P)                             pillars ≥ 0.03 keep rock (VOXEL)
caves    = max( min(E, C, S3, S2), keep )
noodle   = tap noodle( fl(16·(−0.11 − 0.025·Kn + 1.5·max(|An|, |Bn|) + 4·max(0, −Nn))) + cap_n )      (VOXEL combine)
           Nn = interpolated(nb(Nt, −1)), Kn = interpolated(nb(thk_n, 0)), An = interpolated(nb(Ra, 1)), Bn = interpolated(nb(Rb, 1))
nb(N, c) = rangeChoice(y − maxY_n, −1e9, 40, rangeChoice(minY_n − y, −1e9, 40, N, c), c)   noodle noises only within 40 of the band
S_w      = 128·h_w                                                                     §1.4: the wet roof's steep part, at voxel level
final    = max( min( min( tap precave( T + detailG ), caves + S_w ), noodle + S_w ), tap islands(−1e6) )
sky(v)   = rangeChoice(ref body, −1e9, −8, 32, v)                                      deep families skipped ≥ 8 above the surface
cap_f    = G_f·min(1, g_f + g_w + g_i) + 128·min(1, h_f + h_i)                          §1.4: gentle parts g, steep parts h
```

- **As data** (`DEFAULT_DENSITY_TREE`): `defs.body` is the SP5c tree's terrain body (its `root.a.a.x.x`), unchanged, and the root is `max(min(min(tap precave(add(tap terrain(interpolated(ref body)), detailG)), add(caves, ref wetSteep)), add(noodle, ref wetSteep)), tap islands(−1e6))`, written in the order the paths read: `root.a.a.a` the `precave` tap, `root.a.a.a.x.a` the `terrain` tap, `root.a.a.a.x.b` `detailG`, `root.a.a.b.a` the caves slot, `root.a.b.a` the noodle slot, `root.a.a.b.b` and `root.a.b.b` the two `ref wetSteep` (S_w, written once as `defs.wetSteep`, §1.4), `root.b` the islands tap. The `precave` tap names the pre-cave density `T + detailG`, which the cave tag reads (§3.2): no reader can name an untapped node. The caves slot is `max(min(E, C, S3, S2), keep)` with the mins left-nested in that order (`min(min(min(E, C), S3), S2)`), the family taps written in it (`entrances` `root.a.a.b.a.a.a.a.a`, `cheese` `root.a.a.b.a.a.a.a.b`, `spag3d` `root.a.a.b.a.a.a.b`, `spag2d` `root.a.a.b.a.a.b`), and the `noodle` tap is `root.a.b.a`; P is written once, as `defs.pillars`, and `keep` reads it through two `ref pillars` (a tap counts where it is written, so both read one tap). A `rangeChoice` bound is a literal or one parameter ref, so `keep` tests `P·(1/16)` (the exact 0.0625) against `caves.pillars.threshold` instead of P against 16 × 0.03 = 0.48. A tap name is unique, so `detailG` cannot read the `terrain` tap: its `x` is `interpolated(ref body)`, which the compiler CSE-shares with the tap's child (same structure, inside; `src/gen/density/compile.ts:174-187`); the same holds for every read of T inside a slot (`ref body` at the corner) and for the noodle's voxel-level wet roof. Before a family's term lands its slot is `const 1e6`, and `min(v, 1e6)` is `v` for every value a valid tree reaches, so before the first cave terms landed the values, the bounds decisions and the generated world were SP5c's; the noodle slot stays `const 1e6` until the noodle lands.
- `ref body` is CSE-shared by every `interpolated` node (same structure, all inside, `src/gen/density/compile.ts:174-187`), so it is evaluated once per corner. No `interpolated` is nested (`src/gen/density/expr.ts:105`).
- `nb` is lever (3) written into the tree: `cornerFn` evaluates every slot at every corner (`src/gen/density/compile.ts:378-388`) and `rangeChoice` is lazy, so the four noodle noises are not evaluated at corners more than 40 blocks outside the noodle band (40 = `bandSpan`'s static maximum 32 + 8, a literal because a `rangeChoice` bound is a literal or a single ref, `src/gen/density/source.ts:1-12`). Outside it the constants close the tube (toggle 4·1, ridge 1.5·1).
- `spawn` keeps treating the default tree as 2D (`src/gen/column/spawn.ts:50-56`) and `surfaceEst3` searches the `terrain` tap only (`src/gen/column/surfaceEstimate.ts:17`), so neither moves with caves (§3.4).
- The preset trees are re-derived (`src/core/params/presetTrees.ts:80-178`): amplified's six paths move into `defs.body` (`root.a.a.x.x.<rest>` → `defs.body.<rest>`), floating_islands' `root.b.x` keeps its path, and the `was` checks (`:97`) catch a stale path at module load. The island cap is not a preset replacement: it is a term of every tree, set by a leaf (§1.4, D8).
- `beard` stays SP9's (master :677).

### 1.4 Floors, caps and roofs (D2, D4, D7, D8, D19)

- **Family floor (D2):** every family's value is `max(16·value, −F)` with F = 48 (tree literal) before its caps are added. A `max` with a constant bounds the value below whatever the leaves are, so the caps close every family for every leaf value in its static range (the noise leaves' `clampSigma` goes up to 8, `src/core/params/kit.ts:186`, and tier 2 checks at that maximum, `src/gen/density/source.ts:155-165`). Without the floor, spaghetti 3D could reach −70.7 at `clampSigma` 8 and entrances −62.7 at `entrances.threshold` 0.5 (their tube arm; the scaled Ne arm reaches −29.3 at `entrances.offset` −0.5). The floor changes values only where a family is more than 48 below zero, deep inside air, so its shape is unchanged except within a fraction of a block of those cells. The unit test is an interval check over the leaves' static ranges (`bounds.ts` computes the intervals), not a check at the defaults.
- **Surface ramp (D4):** `ramp = clamp((d_s − T)·2, 0, 32)`, d_s = `caves.limits.surfaceDepth` 10 (8 until Task 38). The ramp is 0 where T ≥ 10 (about 10 blocks deep), 20 at the surface (T = 0) and 32 from 6 blocks above it, so the deep families fade out over the top 10 blocks; entrances are the only noise family that reaches the surface. With the cheese roof term (master :675) caverns stay about 12 blocks down. Inside the slots T is read as `ref body` at the corner.
- **Caps: a gentle part, then a steep part (D2).** Each cap is two fractions of a distance outside the region a family may occupy: a gentle part g rising from 0 to 1 over s = `caves.limits.bandSpan` 16 (1 … 32) blocks, then a steep part h rising from 0 to 1 over the next 8 blocks. A family's caps add `cap_f = G_f·min(1, g_f + g_w + g_i) + K·min(1, h_f + h_i)`, K = 128 (tree); the wet roof's steep part K·h_w is added at voxel level, outside the slots (S_w, below). The gentle rise G_f is about the family's typical air depth (its value at the median carved voxel), so over the s blocks the cave shrinks gradually; the steep part guarantees closure: wherever a corner's h ≥ 1/2, the slot is ≥ −48 + 64 > 0. Defaults (tree literals, set from D25's measurement and re-set at tuning): entrances 0.4, cheese 5.8, spaghetti 3D 0.5, spaghetti 2D 0.3, noodle 0.4, the magnitudes, rounded to 0.1, of the median slot values D25 measured at the voxels each family tags (−0.413, −5.836, −0.483, −0.339, −0.397; the first estimate, 16, 16, 2.4, 2.4, 2.4, closed the entrances within 1-2 blocks). A unit test measures each family's closure distance across each cap: the y span over which the carve rate falls from 90 % to 10 % of its value inside the region is ≥ 4 blocks at the defaults; D25 measures it before tuning.
- **Depth bands (D9):** for a family with `minY`, `maxY`: `g_f = clamp((minY − y)·(1/s), 0, 1) + clamp((y − maxY)·(1/s), 0, 1)` and `h_f = clamp((minY − s − y)·(1/8), 0, 1) + clamp((y − maxY − s)·(1/8), 0, 1)`, written with `recip(param …)` as the default tree already writes a param's reciprocal (`src/core/params/defaultTrees.ts:53`). They are not `slide` knots: a knot's y is a literal or a single ref, and the knots must increase over the refs' whole static ranges (`src/gen/density/expr.ts:322`), which the overlapping `minY`/`maxY` ranges (−64 … 320) break. Defaults: cheese −56 … 320, spaghetti 3D −56 … 320, spaghetti 2D −64 … 215, noodle −60 … 64, entrances −64 … 320 (master values where it has them). Spaghetti 2D's layer term is open only where |layerAmp·Nel + 8 − (y + 64)/8| < 0.95 + 0.35·Nthk ≤ 2 (|Nel|, |Nthk| ≤ clampSigma 3), so never above y 208, and at 320 its `maxY` would be dead under U2 (U2's one step of 320 is 272); any `maxY` from 208 to 244 changes no generated output (its cap reaches only corners above y 208, where the layer term is at least 1 and the slot at least 16, and the corners at y 208 are never below 0), and 215 is the highest of them whose U2 lower step (183) has a witness under the band witness scan (§4), so the default is 215 and the world is unchanged by it. The band leaves are ints (y), `bandSpan` an int (blocks). With `minY` > `maxY` the family narrows across the inverted range (every value in it is raised by at least G_f·min(1, (minY − maxY)/s)) and the lint warns (§4); once the inversion is 2s + 8 wide every y lies a band span and 4 blocks inside one of the two caps (h ≥ 1/2), so the family is open nowhere.
- **Wet roof (D7, D19):** with d the depth below the terrain's own surface at that point (`ref body` at a slot's corner, `interpolated(ref body)` at voxel level), `g_w = wet′·clamp((d_w + 8 + s − d)·(1/s), 0, 1)` and `h_w = wet′·clamp((d_w + 8 − d)·(1/8), 0, 1)`, d_w = `caves.limits.waterDepth` 16 (8 … 48). The gentle part g_w is in every family's cap, inside the slots (d = `ref body` at the corner; the noodle's cap at voxel level with d = `interpolated(ref body)`). The steep part is read per position, at voxel level: `S_w = 128·h_w`, with wet′ the position's bilinear `col` readout and d = `interpolated(ref body)`, written once as `defs.wetSteep` and added to the caves slot and to the noodle (`min(min(precave, caves + S_w), noodle + S_w)`, §1.1); inside a slot wet′ is a COLUMN subtree read at the cell's four lattice corners and trilerped (`src/gen/density/reference.ts:10`), so at a shoreline cell whose nearest corner alone is wet the slot's cap is a blend weighted about 1/4 toward wet, which S_w at voxel level does not depend on. S_w ≥ 0, so a voxel open with it is open without it and the tag's candidates are unchanged. Under water, caves are fully open from d_w + 8 + s = 40 blocks below the bed, narrow over the 16 blocks above that, and are closed within d_w + 4 = 20 blocks of the bed (every corner of a voxel's cell at d ≤ 20 gives h_w ≥ 1/2 where wet′ = 1, S_w ≥ 64, and every family is ≥ −48 below it). The depth is the density's, not `offset`'s: the bed lies at `offset + jag·J + sigma·slide(overhang)` (`src/core/params/defaultTrees.ts:43-66`), and with sigma 3 at sea and 12-26 on coasts (`src/core/params/shapeDefaults.ts:23-26`) it can lie 9 to more than 30 blocks below `offset`.
- **wet′** = `clamp(4·wetMask, 0, 1)`, `wetMask` the finite `col` field that is 1 at a lattice point whose `surfaceWaterLevel` is finite and 0 elsewhere (D19; in `SAMPLE_FIELDS`, bounds [0, 1] in `COL_FIELD_BOUNDS`, `src/core/constants.ts`). Water v0 fills air only in `base − 12 < y ≤ waterTop` with `waterTop` read at the nearest quart corner (`src/gen/pipeline/terrainStage.ts:98-118`, `src/gen/column/columnStage.ts` `nearestCornerIndex`), and `waterLevel` is the sea level 63 for `offset` < 63, a river's wet flag or a lake (`src/gen/column/columnPoint.ts:51-54`, `src/core/constants.ts:7`). So every position water v0 fills has wet′ = 1 by construction: a voxel's quart offsets t ∈ {0, ¼, ½, ¾} make the bilinear readout of 0/1 values exact, the nearest corner's weight is ≥ 1/4, and that corner is wet. A unit scan, `wetCover`, checks over 100,000 positions of the quick tier's seeds on default and archipelago that every position whose water v0 `waterTop` ≥ y −64 has wet′ = 1 bit for bit at the position, which is what S_w reads; a miss is a defect. The field realises D7 exactly: caves stay about 16 blocks under every water bed and dry land's caves are untouched. (`riverStrength`, `s·s_alt` in `src/gen/column/rivers.ts:77`, is a coast fade times an altitude fade, not a distance to a river: as a wet term it marks about 93 % of land, measured at seed 42, and closes every land entrance; and a margin on `offset` cannot cover bilinear `offset` between a sea corner and a land corner, which left 28 sea-shore misses after the widenings 4 → 8 and 65 → 67 and a `riverDist` term.) In a basin `offset` is the basin floor (`src/gen/column/lakes.ts:68-69`), and a lake's lattice points are wet, so the roof contains the master's lake roof.
- **Island cap (D8):** `g_i = clamp((y − capTop + 8 + s)·(1/s), 0, 1)`, `h_i = clamp((y − capTop + 8)·(1/8), 0, 1)`, capTop = `caves.limits.capTop` (an int y, 180 … 344; default 344, above the world's top y 319, so it has no effect: g_i and h_i are 0 at every corner up to y 320 while `bandSpan` ≤ 16; 180 in floating_islands' overlay, `src/core/params/profiles.ts:34-37`). In floating_islands, caves narrow over y 156 … 172 and every corner at y ≥ 176 has h_i ≥ 1/2, so no noise cave air lies at y ≥ 176; carvers shrink to nothing by y 172 (§2.4). No cave air at y ≥ 180 = `ISLAND_RUN_MIN_BOTTOM − 1` (`src/core/constants.ts:94`), so no ground run gets its bottom at y ≥ 181 from a cave, which meets SP3e's hand-over by construction (D31 for the surface predicate). `C7.islandCapAir` counts cave air at y ≥ 176 in a floating_islands region: 0 (§10.1).

## 2. Carvers (D5, D21, D35)

### 2.1 Sources and the window

- Per source chunk (sx, sz) and kind k ∈ {worm, ravine, underground worm (cut line)}: `rng = new Xoshiro128(hash2(deriveSeed(seed, 'caves.' + k), sx, sz))` (`src/core/rng.ts:17-66`, `src/core/hash.ts:28-36`, `:262`); the first `nextFloat()` against the kind's chance (worm 0.15, ravine 0.015, underground worm 0.07; master :702-703); then the path's draws in the fixed order of §2.2-§2.3. The first RNG consumer in `gen/caves/`; `src/gen/surface/bands.ts:13-19` already seeds a `Xoshiro128` from `deriveSeed` the same way.
- **Window (D21):** a source's carved voxels lie within L_max + r_max of its start, and the start lies inside its chunk, so a source in chunk sx reaches the chunks sx − R … sx + R with R the largest integer such that 16·R < 16 + L_max + r_max. With L_max = 160 (the length leaves' maximum, §4) and r_max = 9.5 (the largest horizontal radius along a path that any leaf combination allows: (1.5 + 3)·2.0 for worms and underground worms, 1.5 + 8 for ravines, whose width factor only narrows them, §2.3; a room, up to 7·2.0 = 14, sits at the start, where 14 < 16 + 160): 16 + 169.5 = 185.5, so R = 11, 23 × 23 = 529 sources. A path's points lie within its length of its start (each step moves 1 block; a branch starts at the trunk's midpoint with at most the remaining half). `C7.reachOverflow` checks it on 100,000 sources per kind, at the defaults and with every carver leaf at its static maximum, on each path's voxel reach (its box: every ellipsoid's centre widened by its own rh and rv), not on its centre line (§10.1). Cost estimate: 529 rolls × 50 ns ≈ 26 µs per column; about 79 worm, 8 ravine and 37 underground-worm paths per window at the default chances.

### 2.2 Worm and underground-worm paths

- Start: x = 16·sx + 16·u, z = 16·sz + 16·u, y = startYMin + (startYMax − startYMin)·u (worms −56 … 180, master :708; underground worms −56 … 24); yaw = 2π·u; pitch = (u − 0.5)/4.
- Steps n = max(1, length − ⌊lengthVar·u⌋) (112 − rand(28), master :708; the max keeps a path when the leaves allow `lengthVar` ≥ `length`); thickness t = 2·u + u; hMul ∈ [radiusMin, radiusMax] = [0.7, 1.4], vMul ∈ [0.8, 1.3], floor ∈ [−1, −0.4] (master :709-710).
- Per step i: rh = (1.5 + detSin(π·i/n)·t)·hMul, rv = (1.5 + detSin(π·i/n)·t)·vMul: both ends taper to 1.5·mul, so a worm ends in a rounded cap, never larger than the steps before it (`C7.endTaper`); a start room (below) is the path's first ellipsoid and may be larger than the steps after it, so the start end is the first step ellipsoid after the room; position += (cos p·cos y, sin p, cos p·sin y) through `detCos`/`detSin` (`src/core/detMath.ts:21-85`); pitch ·= 0.7; yaw += 0.1·yd; pitch += 0.1·pd; yd = 0.75·yd + (u − u)·u·4; pd = 0.9·pd + (u − u)·u·2 (Minecraft's drift; the constants are leaves under `caves.worms.shape`, §4, so master §0.5 holds).
- Branch: at step ⌊n/2⌋, with chance 0.25 (master :711) and t > 1, the trunk ends and two branches start there with yaw ± π/2, thickness 0.5 + 0.5·u, n − ⌊n/2⌋ steps, from the same RNG stream. The trunk's end lies inside the branches' starts, so it is not a visible end.
- Room (D35): with chance 0.25 a worm starts with an ellipsoid of horizontal radius (1 + 6·u)·hMul and vertical radius that times yScale ∈ [0.1, 0.9] (master's yScale, :709; Minecraft's room).
- Underground worms use the worm leaves for everything but their chance, their start range and `maxY` (§4), and shrink to nothing between maxY − 16 = 24 and maxY = 40 (§2.4), so none reaches above y 40 and none ends on a flat ceiling.
- Output: a `CarverPath` of Float64Array ellipsoids (x, y, z, rh, rv, floor) with its box.

### 2.3 Ravine paths

Start as worms with y ∈ [10, 72] (master :714); yaw 2π·u; pitch (u − 0.5)·0.25 (vertical rotation ±0.125); thickness from a trapezoid on [0, thicknessMax 6] with plateau 2; n = lengthMin + ⌊(max(lengthMin, lengthMax) − lengthMin + 1)·u⌋ ∈ [84, 112]; rh = 1.5 + detSin(π·i/n)·t, rv = rh·3 (yScale 3); per-y width factors redrawn every 3 blocks (width smoothness 3): f = 1 + u·u, stored as f² per world y (384 values in the path); the voxel test multiplies the horizontal term by f²(y) ≥ 1 (Minecraft's form), which narrows the ravine at some heights and roughens its walls, so its horizontal reach never exceeds rh. Ravines taper at both ends like worms, so none is "cut short" (`C7.endTaper`).

### 2.4 Carving a column (D6, D7, D8)

For each source in the window whose path box meets the column box, for each ellipsoid whose box meets it, for each voxel in both boxes: `d = ((x + 0.5 − cx)/rh)²·w(y) + ((z + 0.5 − cz)/rh)²·w(y) + ((y + 0.5 − cy)/rv)²` (w = 1 for worms, the ravine's f² otherwise), in that operation order; carve when `d < 1 − σ(x, y, z)` and, for worms, `(y + 0.5 − cy)/rv > floor`. The shrink `σ = max(σ_w, σ_i, σ_u)`, each in [0, 1] and linear, so a carver narrows to nothing over a span instead of stopping on a plane:
- `σ_w = wet′·clamp((d_w + 8 + s − d)·(1/(8 + s)), 0, 1)` with d = the target position's **density top** − y, so carvers close where the noise caves close (§1.4's wet roof). The density top is the position's highest solid voxel after the density fill, before carving (y −64, the bedrock, never counts; −65 when there is none); with `islands.enabled` it is the top of the position's highest solid run whose bottom lies below `ISLAND_RUN_MIN_BOTTOM` (water v0's base rule, `src/gen/pipeline/terrainStage.ts` `columnWaterV0`), because an island's top over the sea would make σ_w 0 under the island and open carvers right under the sea bed, where water v0 fills them (island runs never shelter the sea under them). It is apart from the harness's pre-cave top (the highest voxel solid or tagged, which an entrance breaching the top raises). One `densityTopOf` (`src/gen/caves/carve.ts`) computes it for T, `carvedAt` and the carver reference;
- `σ_i = clamp((y − capTop + 8 + s)·(1/s), 0, 1)`: 1 from y 172 when capTop is 180;
- `σ_u = clamp((y − maxY_u + 16)·(1/16), 0, 1)` for underground worms only.
The path stays a pure function of its source; the shrink reads only the target column's sample and its density fill, which T already holds. Only solid voxels are carved: at the carve step (§3.1) T has only `SOLID`, and every state T can produce there is `CARVABLE` (`src/world/blocks/registry.ts:223`, `:260`; only `packed_ice` is not, and the surface pass places it later), so the rule holds and is kept in the code for SP9's structure blocks. `aquifer.fluidAt` (master :719) is SP7's; until then a carved voxel is air.

### 2.5 The path cache

An LRU of 1024 `CarverPath`s per worker (`CARVER_CACHE_PATHS`, `src/core/constants.ts`: gen exports no numeric data) keyed by (kind, sx, sz), held in the GenContext's memo (a WeakMap, as `surfaceContextOf`, `src/gen/surface/context.ts:115-118`), cleared with the context on `configure`. A miss recomputes the same path (pure), so the cache never changes output (`DT1.carverCache`, §10.3). A column needs about 124 paths; neighbours share most of them. Memory: a worm path holds up to 112 ellipsoids × 6 doubles (about 5.4 KB), a ravine about 8 KB with its 384 f² values, so the LRU holds at most about 8.2 MB per worker (all ravines) and about 5.5 MB typically; building a path allocates, so `terrainHeap`'s garbage budget (about 1.5 MB per column, `src/gen/column/lakes.ts:8-11`) is re-measured with caves at D25's second checkpoint.

## 3. The T stage, the cave tag and the probes

### 3.1 Order (D6)

Density fill → **carve** (new; one `stop()` poll before it; it first takes each position's density top from the fill's solidity, for §2.4's wet shrink) → **tag** (§3.2) → water v0 → per-block surface biome → surface pass → 24 `setProto` sections → aux A and B (today `src/gen/pipeline/terrainStage.ts:141-184`; the carve slot is between `:144` and `:145`). The scan skins the carved column: a ravine floor open to the sky is the sky run and gets the biome's top; cave floors are lower runs and get stone or deepslate (rules 1-2); grass never appears below the sky-open run (master :798). `test/unit/terrainStage.test.ts:420`'s poll count gains the carve poll.

### 3.2 The tag (D17, D20)

`CAVE_KIND` (in `src/core/constants.ts`, with `CAVE_FILM_KIND` 15, since gen exports no numeric data): 0 none, 1 cheese, 2 spaghetti 3D, 3 spaghetti 2D, 4 noodle, 5 entrance, 6 worm, 7 ravine, 8 underground worm, 9 other density air (air that no family tap present in the tree explains); 10-15 reserved (the mesh's film uses 15 in the X-ray pack, never in the store); the high 4 bits reserved, 0. The rule, a pure function of the voxel:
1. if the final block is solid, 0;
2. else if the `precave` tap's value `T + detailG ≤ 0` (not pre-cave solid), 0;
3. else if the density made it air: the candidates are E, C, S3 and S2 when `caves ≤ 0` (which needs `keep ≤ 0`), and the noodle when `noodle ≤ 0`; the tag is the candidate with the lowest value, ties in the order E, C, S3, S2, noodle. A voxel inside pillar rock (keep > 0) that a noodle opens is therefore a noodle, never the lowest of the four families that `keep` overrides;
4. else (a carver carved it) the kind of the first source, in the window's fixed row order, whose ellipsoid carved it.

Where the values come from (`fillDensityColumn`, `src/gen/density/bounds.ts:271-282`): a cell decided before its corners is decided by T + detailG alone, since the cave taps' intervals straddle 0 everywhere underground; one decided air there is sky, and its voxels get 0 without evaluation (a unit test asserts no such cell holds a pre-cave-solid voxel). A cell evaluated voxel by voxel has the family values from `voxelFn`. A cell decided air after its corners (a cave interior, which `voxelFn` skips) has its corners, and the tag trilerps the family slots there per voxel; that work is in §1.6's voxel estimate. The values are read through a new compiled `tapReader(name)`, resolved once per compile, which reads a tap's value at the voxel registers that `voxelFn` (or `atVoxel`) has set, with no Map lookup and no epoch bump (`tapFn` does both per call, `src/gen/density/compile.ts:419-425`, which would defeat the memo of shared subterms); a tap inside `interpolated` (only in an edited tree) is read as `tapFn` reads it, one epoch per corner, and then sets the voxel registers again.

**Trees without the family taps** (a user-edited tree, D36): with no `precave` tap, step 2 reads the `terrain` tap (pre-cave solid where T > 0); with neither, density air gets 0 (pre-cave solidity is unknown) and only carver kinds are tagged; with some family taps missing, the candidates of step 3 are the taps present, and density air that none of them explains gets 9. DT2's `tagProbe` runs on `treeMutator` trees too.

The harness's "debug channel" (master :678) is this channel; the harness adds only a pillar bit (rock kept by `keep` where `min(E, C, S3, S2) < 0`), recomputed per voxel by `caveDebugColumn` for C3's pillars part (which also gives, apart from the channel, the `terrain` tap's value per voxel for C7.switchWall).

### 3.3 Store, padded view, memory (D20)

A third proto channel `cave` (u8 per voxel) written by `setProto` (`src/world/store/api.ts:63`), shared into the final set by reference count as blocks and fluid are (`src/world/store/store.ts:432-433`), read by `ColumnView.sectionCave`, copied into the padded view as a fourth array (`src/world/store/padded.ts:38-41`). Uniform-0 sections cost no slot (`src/world/store/section.ts:3-6`). The column record is fixed today: `RECORD_INTS` 160, `REC_FINAL` 16 + 24 × 4 and `REC_PROTO` 112 + 24 × 2 (`src/world/store/columnTable.ts:24-45`). With a cave entry in both sets the final set takes 24 × 5, `REC_PROTO` becomes 136, the proto set 24 × 3, and `RECORD_INTS` 208 (the column table grows from 2.6 to 3.4 MB). The sites that walk the entries change with it: the proto-to-final share (`store.ts:432-433`), `replaceBlocks` (`:585-590`, used by D's diff overlay and the sim's edits), which keeps the cave entry, and the release path (`:688-692`); a unit test checks that an edited section keeps its cave entry. Edits leave the channel: a placed block makes the voxel solid (the X-ray ignores it), a dug voxel was solid and so has tag 0 (not cave). It is not persisted (T regenerates it; diffs hold blocks only). `regionHash` covers it, so DT1 and the `*.region.T.*` goldens check it.

### 3.4 Probes (D22)

`solidAt(x, y, z)` = `probe` (`src/gen/density/probe.ts:19-26`) > 0 and not `carvedAt(x, y, z)`; `carvedAt` runs §2.4's test over the window's paths (same code as `carveColumn`); `caveTagAt` runs §3.2 at one voxel. The surface probe's column rebuild (`src/gen/surface/probe.ts`) carves through `carveColumn`, or `surfaceProbeBulk` fails. **Spawn and landing:** `spawnY` keeps the 2D estimate under the default tree (`src/gen/column/spawn.ts:50-56`, compared canonically), so `sp2a.spawn` is unchanged; `landingSurface` reads `surfaceEst3` on the `terrain` tap (`spawn.ts:63-69`). Neither sees an entrance or a ravine, so a new world or a teleport over one starts on the pre-cave surface: in flight the player hovers there, in walk mode falls to the floor once the column is published (accepted; master §3.7).

## 5. The X-ray (D10, D23)

### 5.1 The mesh pass

- `meshXray(padded, cave, builder)` in `src/mesh/xray.ts`, called by `meshColumnPayload` after `meshFluid` (`src/mesh/column.ts:304-336`). A voxel is **hollow** when its tag's kind ≠ 0 and its block is not an opaque full cube. Emitted: each face of a hollow voxel whose neighbour is an opaque full face (a cave wall), kind = the hollow's tag; and each up face of an opaque or fluid voxel whose up neighbour is air with sky light 15 (the surface film, kind 15; an overhang or a canopy leaves a gap in it, accepted in D10). Greedy merge on (kind, face) only (no light, no AO), so quads merge better than terrain quads (`src/mesh/faces.ts:111`).
- `PACK_LAYOUT` gains a variant `xray`: position as the solid word's, data word face (3 bits), kind (4 bits), 4 bits reserved for SP6b's cave biome (`src/mesh/packing.ts:2-4`).
- Pass 3 everywhere the pass list is fixed at three: `column.ts:37-39`, `:83`, `:265`; `MeshPass` (`src/mesh/builder.ts:10`); `regionBatch.ts:37`, `:119-123`, `:170`, `:219-220` (`sortObjects` off for pass 3); `regionMesh.ts:37`, `:192`, `:403`, `:593` (no translucent sort for pass 3); `src/workers/protocol.ts:568`, `:615`; the stand-ins that compile each pass's program at load, `addStandIns` (`src/render/regionGrid.ts:551-559`, whose pass list names passes 0-2), which gains pass 3; the staging reservation (`src/render/regionGrid.ts:38-42`) 16,384 vertices for pass 3 (estimate; `xrayVertexBytes` in L14 records the real use). The opaque reservation, 131,072 vertices per region, was sized without cave walls, and a region that outgrows it re-creates its buffers ×1.5 (`regionGrid.ts:44`) on the main thread while streaming: D25's second checkpoint re-measures every pass's use with the carvers and the X-ray pass, and the reservations are resized from those measurements right after push B. The X-ray commit cannot resize them: it lies between push A and push B (§12.3), before the carvers exist, so pass 3 starts at the 16,384-vertex estimate. L13 and L14 count growth events.

### 5.2 The material

`xray.glsl` (`render/materials/**`, the only GLSL boundary, master :179), in the `batch` and `mesh` variants: flat colour from a 16-entry palette uniform by kind × FACE_SHADE; alpha 0.14 for walls, 0.05 for the film; additive blending, `depthWrite: false`, `depthTest: false`, double-sided, `forceSinglePass`, so the result needs no sorting; a distance fade to 0 at the fog end ((RD − 1)·16, `src/render/materials/index.ts:54-64`); wall fragments outside `[yMin, yMax + 1)` and every fragment beyond `xrayRadius` are discarded; the film (kind 15) ignores the band (D10). It reads no lightmap, so the light floor and night vision do not touch it. Compiled at load through the section renderers' stand-ins (`addStandIns` in `src/render/regionGrid.ts`, with pass 3 among its passes; `compileScene`, `src/render/renderer.ts:325-343`). The palette's colours are D10's.

### 5.3 Toggle, band, background

- One uniform `xrayOn` in the shared uniform objects (`src/render/materials/uniforms.ts:3-7`): the X-ray vertex shader collapses every vertex while it is 0; the terrain (opaque, cutout) and water vertex shaders collapse every vertex while it is 1; the ghost (the terrain shader under `#ifdef GHOST`, `src/render/materials/glsl/terrain.glsl.ts:121-123`, excluded from the collapse), the selection box and the outline are untouched. A toggle writes uniforms and the clear colour only (O(1), no allocation; `zeroAlloc.test.ts` covers it).
- X-ray on: sky dome and clouds hidden, fog off for the terrain passes (they draw nothing), clear colour #0b0e14.
- Band: `yMin`, `yMax` uniforms; default all (−64 … 319); PageUp/PageDown move a band of 32 by 8 (from "all", the first press makes the band 32 blocks centred on the camera's y); Home returns to all. Radius: `xrayRadius` = the fog end unless L14 forces a smaller one (D29).

### 5.4 With live regeneration

A cave edit regenerates every column from T (`coordinator.ts:1505-1506`); each new payload carries its pass 3 rows, swapped in atomically with the column's other passes (SP4c's swap, `RegionBackend.swap` in `src/render/regionGrid.ts`, in the pump that uploads the replacement's last row), so the caves grow, shrink and move in place while the X-ray is on. Band, radius and colour mode are uniforms; nothing remeshes.

### 5.5 Cost (each estimate measured by L14 or the bench)

Mesh time +25-50 % of `meshSolid` per non-trivial section (`mesh.xray` row); geometry 30-60 % of the cave-wall quads plus the film, about +5-15 MB at RD 12 against SP5c's 39.0 MB geometry (gate: GPU estimate ≤ 150 MB); draw calls +10-20 (SP5c max 16 of 100, `drawCallsMax` in `docs/superpowers/specs/assets/sp5c/bench-iris-chrome.json`); vertex work for the collapsed passes on either side of the toggle; fill (additive overdraw) bounded by the band, the fade and the discard. ## 6. Light floor and night vision (D14, D15)

### 6.1 The light floor

In `lightmapRgba` only (`src/render/lightmap.ts:41-57`): after the per-channel max of the sky and block terms and before the brightness lift, `c′ = F + (1 − F)·c` with `F = LIGHT_FLOOR = 0.02`, neutral grey. ### 6.2 Night vision

## 8. Failure modes and review focus

- **A flat wall where a switch or a limit lies** (a refactor reintroducing a hard switch): §1.3's continuity unit test; `C7.verticalStep`, `C7.switchWall`, `C7.flatWallShare`.
- **A cap too steep** (continuous, but closing a cave within a block of a plane): §1.4's closure-distance unit test; `C7.verticalStep` across the band edges; `C7.flatWallShare` on a sea-floor region.
- **A carver truncated at a chunk border or at the window:** `C7.reachOverflow` (pure), `C7.carverReference` against a global reference, `C7.borderPlaneRatio`.
- **A carver that depends on its target column** (Minecraft's `canReach` shortcut, or a cache read with state): no per-target logic in the path builder (§2.1); `DT1.carverCache`, `DT1.isolated`.
- **Swiss-cheese surface:** `C9.holedSurface`, `C9.thinRoof`, `C9.entrancesMax`; the ramp (§1.4).
- **No entrances near the player:** `C2`, `C9.nearestEntrance`.
- **A repeated look:** `C8.aperiodicity` (lags from 16 blocks, so chunk-sized rhythms show), `C8.typeMixVariation`; the carver starts' uniformity is a unit test.
- **Water walls in caves** (D7's roof too weak at a shore, or a wet position the roof misses): the `wetCover` unit scan; `C7.waterWall` gated on default and archipelago. B5's master part "lake water with air horizontally adjacent" was never built (`test/metrics/water.metric.ts:90-104`) and stays SP7's (master :1905).
- **Families below the cap's reach** (a leaf moved to its extreme): the family floor (§1.4) and its interval check over the static ranges; `C7.islandCapAir`.
- **Worlds saved before SP6a opening without noise caves:** D36's migration and its tests (§12.4).
- **The tag disagreeing with the blocks** (a cell decided air by bounds left untagged, or a noodle in pillar rock named after another family): the tag unit test (carved set = pre-cave solid ∧ air on every voxel of 64 columns; a noodle through pillar rock); `DT2.tagProbe`, also on `treeMutator` trees.
- **T over budget, or the fill over 6 s:** D25's prototype, its second checkpoint and §1.6's levers; `terrain.real`, `terrain.islands`, L-fill.
- **A slow slice or finder:** §7.4's corner memo and the `slice.caves128` gate; §7.3's budget and `finder.nearest`.
- **Stutter walking in caves or with the X-ray on:** L13, L14 (`over33` 0).
- **The X-ray hiding nothing or everything on toggle** (rows uploaded only on first draw): D23's uniform collapse; L14's toggle segment; a smoke step.
- **A first toggle's long frame** (all pending rows uploaded at once): rows always drawn (collapsed), so none pend; L14's `xrayToggleMaxMs`.
- **Night vision touching stored light:** the unit test that toggling posts no worker message and leaves `litHash` unchanged; L1, L3 and DT1.light unchanged by construction.
- **The light floor flattening night:** §6.1's ratio test; the user's eye at the check.
- **Preset trees broken by the new root:** the `was` checks at module load (`presetTrees.ts:97`); the `sp3e.region.T.*` goldens re-recorded under the same bump.
- **Grass at sky 0 under cave ceilings:** S1 `grassSky0` gated, with a floating_islands window holding a large island over grass (SP4a's hand-over, its spec :737).
- **A cave-only Apply waiting for the map's watchdog:** §4's test.
- **CI's wall growing past 3 min 30 s:** §11's levers.
