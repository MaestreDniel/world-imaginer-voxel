## Points for the user

Choices only the user can make, each changing what gets built. Both were answered on 2026-10-29 with the recommendation, which this spec is written to: Point 1 "Cada vez que activas el modo", Point 2 "Un mundo sin guardar".
1. **How often the automatic backup is made.** The brief: "the first terrain change in a world with builds keeps the original as a copy". "First" can mean the first in the world's life, or the first each time you switch the modification mode on. **Recommendation:** each time you switch the mode on, the first terrain change made while the world has builds keeps a copy ("My world (before the change)", then "My world (before the change 2)", …), so every terrain experiment can be undone by opening its copy; a change made while the world has no builds makes no copy and does not use up that mode session's copy; you delete copies you no longer want from Load world. **Alternative:** one copy per world, ever; a later experiment, after more building, has no copy. **What the choice changes:** §3.6's latch (per mode session, or a `backupMade` flag in the `worlds` record), E6's `backupSpurious` and `backupMissing` (§6.1), and the §2.7 amendment's wording (§9).
2. **What a link opens.** Today the map's "Open in 3D" opens `/?at=<x>,<z>#<the map's world>` (`open3dHref`, `src/ui/map/toolbar.ts:53-59`), and the tools open `/?rd=…` (`src/ui/world/startScreen.ts:14-20`). **Recommendation:** these keep opening an unsaved world exactly as in SP5a (live map edits, nothing saved), with a notice that stays until dismissed: "This world is not saved: it was opened from a link. Use New world on the start screen to keep one." The bench, the smoke and `measureChrome.ts` keep working unchanged. **Alternative:** "Open in 3D" creates a saved world from the map's seed, preset and parameters (named after the seed) and opens it; the tools' queries stay unsaved.

The first draft's Point 3 (a way back to the worlds menu) is now a decision, D31. What decides when a failure criterion's threshold is missed is settled from the brief in D28: the user's own check.

## Goal

The world is kept. `/` opens a start screen (New world · Load world · Settings). New world takes a name, a preset and a seed (random, typed, or a dice); Load world lists your worlds newest first with Play, Rename, Export and Delete, and imports a `.wiworld`. Play opens a world exactly as you left it: its blocks, where you stand and look, walking or flying and the flying speed, the time of day, the hotbar and its slot, the block undo and redo history, and its saved places. Nothing has a save button: the world saves itself every 2 s and when the tab is hidden or closed. One world opens in one tab at a time. Settings (from the start screen, or G in the world) has a Graphics and a Controls tab, shared by every world. In the world, B saves a place (a name and one of 8 colours); the map shows places as coloured dots and lists them in a Places tab, where a click teleports. Terrain changes from the map need the world-modification mode, off whenever a world opens; the first terrain change in a world with builds keeps the world before it as a backup copy. A world saved with an older generator opens with today's terrain, with a notice. Underneath: a `persist/` layer over IndexedDB with a `MemoryKV` for tests, the master's `ColumnDiffV1` codec, a single-transaction flush from the sim worker, the `stateTable` remap, and `.wiworld`. Measured by E1-E4, E5's flush part, E6 (redefined here) and E7, and by the brief's failure criteria turned into gates where they can be (§6.2). The generated world does not change (`GENERATOR_VERSION` 5, the 59 goldens byte-identical).

## Non-goals

- **Styling** of every new screen: the user, "el diseño/estilo en el frontend lo abordaré mejor una vez acabado todo". The screens reuse the existing start-screen and help classes (`src/ui/styles.css:85-102`); styling is not a cut-line item.
- **The selection tool and its clipboard:** SP5c (§10).
- **Keeping an older generator's terrain**, a copy or a choice when a world was saved with an older generator (the brief: "No copy, no choice dialog, no attempt to keep the old terrain").
- **Moving builds** after a terrain change: blocks stay at their coordinates (SP5a D14; the brief).
- **A separate JSON or CSV export of places** ("no separate JSON/CSV export unless asked").
- **The preset library** (master §2.7's `presets` store; SP2b spec :502), **touchpad sensitivity** (SP4a spec :729) and **"duplicate" worlds** (SP4a spec :727): not in the brief. The first two go to SP10 (§10); duplicate is covered by Export/Import and the backup copy and goes nowhere.
- **Settings that do not exist yet** (fog distance, clouds, shadows, the quality preset): shown greyed out, built by SP11. Audio settings: SP8c.

### Technical decisions (no visible effect)

- **D16 Five IndexedDB stores, split by how often they change** (§2.1). `wi10` version 1: `worlds` (the record, with the full parameters, written at create, rename, Apply, the older-generator open, import, and at an open whose `stateTable` differs from today's, D22), `state` (player, time, hotbar, places, `flushSeq`, `updated`: a few KB, written by every flush that has a change), `history` (the block history, written only when it changed), `diffs` (`[worldId, colKey]` → `ColumnDiffV1` bytes) and `meta` (empty in SP5b; SP5c's clipboard may live there). Edited `json` trees reach 128 KiB of canonical text each (master §2.7), so writing the parameters every 2 s, as master §4.13's "the `worlds` record (player, time, `flushSeq`)" reads, would clone them for nothing; the history can grow large with SP5c's pastes. `packs` and `presets` are not created now: SP8c and SP10 add them with a version bump. Alternative: the master's single `worlds` record per flush.
- **D17 `persist/` on both threads** (§2.1, §3.1). The worlds menu, which runs before any sim worker exists, reads and writes IndexedDB on the main thread through the same `persist/idb.ts`; during play every write of the open world goes through the sim worker, its single writer (master §4.1). Master §1's "persist/idb.ts, which runs in the sim worker" gains "and on the main thread for the worlds menu". The arch rules already allow it: `ui` value-imports every layer (`test/arch/rules/imports.ts:31-32`), `indexedDB` is allowed only in `persist/idb.ts` (`test/arch/rules/banned.ts:210-211`). Alternative: a menu worker (a second IDB thread for no gain).
- **D18 The sim worker flushes** (§3.2, §3.3). The main thread posts the small state every 2 s; the sim writes its dirty columns, the state and, when posted, the history in one readwrite transaction; one flush in flight, a later tick merged into the next. The sim already orders messages, so an edit posted before a flush is in it (`src/workers/simHandler.ts:1-17`). The handler is synchronous today (`handle(m): SimReply`, `simHandler.ts:32-36`; the in-process harness posts its result at once, `test/harness/inProcessSim.ts:31-32`); it gains deferred replies for `load`, `flush` and `backup`: a message's synchronous part runs when it arrives (a flush takes and encodes its dirty set then), its reply is posted when the transaction settles, and until `load` has settled every later message waits in arrival order. Alternative: flush from the main thread (needs a mirror of the DiffStore there, and an edit is known only after its reply).
- **D19 A world's diffs load whole at open** (§3.4). The sim reads every `diffs` record of the world in one key-range read, decodes and remaps it into the DiffStore, and answers the set of edited columns before the first D; nothing is prefetched later and nothing is evicted (SP5a already keeps a session's diffs in memory; master §4.2's "the column's diff stays in the sim worker's DiffStore" holds). Master §4.13's `getAllKeys` index and T-ring prefetch are replaced. Its memory is the DiffStore's nested `Map`s (`src/sim/diffStore.ts:31-32`), an estimated 50-80 B per entry in V8 (not measured; `persist.metric.ts` records the heap with the heavy save, §6.2), so one dense section is about 0.3 MB. Alternative: the master's prefetch (needed only when a world's edits outgrow memory; SP5c bounds its pastes, §10).
- **D20 The edited-column set travels as changes** (§3.3). Today every `edited` reply carries the whole set of edited columns (`src/sim/edits.ts:421`) and the client rebuilds its set from it (`src/engine/simClient.ts:112-113`); a saved world's set is its lifetime's, paid on every click. The `loaded` reply carries the whole set once; `edited` carries `entryAdded` and `entryRemoved`. Alternative: keep the whole set (linear in the world's edited columns per click).
- **D21 `ColumnDiffV1` exactly as master §2.7** (§2.2), its field widths fixed here: `v` a u8, the genKey two u32 with the low word first (`Hash64` is `[lo, hi]`, `src/core/hash.ts:2-3`). The trailer is written with count 0 (no fluid ticks before SP7); any count decodes, and SP5b drops decoded ticks (the DiffStore has no place for them, `src/sim/diffStore.ts:31-87`). The header's genKey is the world's genKey when the column was written and is never checked on load (D8: entries apply under any generator). Under a new generator every entry is kept, one equal to the newly generated value included (master §2.7's Rebase rule, which dropped those, goes with the fork dialog). Alternative: drop the header's genKey (a format change against the master).
- **D22 `stateTable` remap on every load, the stored table kept equal to the ids on disk** (§2.3). A world stores `stateTable` as the state keys `stateKey(s)` for `s < stateCount` (`src/world/blocks/registry.ts:119-121`); loading or importing maps each stored id through it to today's id; a key that no longer parses becomes air with a count in the notice "n blocks of unknown types became air" (master §2.2). The history's values remap the same way; the hotbar is by type name and needs none (`src/player/hotbar.ts:67-92`). Every flush writes today's ids, so the stored table must describe them: when the stored table differs from today's, the sim writes, before answering `loaded` and in one transaction, `worlds` with today's table and, when the stored table is not a prefix of today's (a state removed or renamed; ids are otherwise append-only, master §2.2), every column re-encoded under today's ids and the remapped history. A world never holds ids of two tables. Alternative: a `stateTable` per column record (no rewrite at open, a larger record per column); and, for an unknown state, refusing the world instead of air.
- **D23 `.wiworld` as master §2.7, its header fixed here** (§2.4); E2's hash reads the decompressed content, never the gzip bytes (`CompressionStream`'s output is not specified byte for byte across engines).
- **D24 Web Locks for one tab per world** (§3.5). The browser releases a lock when its tab closes or crashes; the page releases its own on `pagehide`, after posting the last save, and a page restored from the back/forward cache (`pageshow` with `persisted`) reloads. The lock is injected, so unit tests drive it (Node 24.21 has `navigator.locks`, checked with `node -e`). Alternative: a BroadcastChannel ping (cannot tell a crashed tab from a slow one).
- **D25 The mode as a gate in the session** (§3.6). `WorldSession` gains `setWritable(on)`: while off, its value mutators (`setSeedText`, `newSeed`, `setProfile`, `set`, `reset`, `undo`, `redo`, `load`; `src/engine/session.ts:205-294`) change nothing and notify nothing, while `beginGesture`, `endGesture` and `subscribe` work as before; `setWritable(false)` first ends an open gesture (a drag cut short would otherwise leave `inGesture` true, and the apply control would never apply again, `src/ui/world/applyControl.ts:104`). ## 2. Storage

### 2.1 IndexedDB `wi10` version 1 (D16, D17)

| store | key | value |
|---|---|---|
| `worlds` | `id` | `{id, name, seedText, seed: [lo, hi], profile, params, schemaVersion, paramsHash, generatorVersion, genKey, stateTable: string[], created, backupOf: string \| null}` |
| `state` | `worldId` | `{worldId, flushSeq, updated, player: {x, y, z, yaw, pitch, mode, speed} \| null, time: {t, speedIndex}, hotbar: {v: 1, active, slots}, places: Place[]}` |
| `history` | `worldId` | `{worldId, done: Step[], undone: Step[]}`, each ≤ 100 steps |
| `diffs` | `[worldId, colKey]` | `ColumnDiffV1` bytes (`Uint8Array`) |
| `meta` | string | empty in SP5b |

- `params` is the full parameter document (master §2.7), loaded as D33 says (`migrate` → `applyPatch` over `resolveProfile(profile)`); the session opens with the record's `seedText`, `profile` and `diffParams(schema, resolveProfile(profile), params)` (`kit.ts:484`) as its `SessionSnapshot` (`src/engine/session.ts:42-46`). `genKey` and `paramsHash` are 16 hex digits. `player.y` is the eye (`src/player/fly.ts:18-20`); `speed` 1-96 (`fly.ts:30-33`); `time.t` in [0, 1) and `speedIndex` an index of `TIME_SPEEDS` (`dayNight.ts:15`). `Place = {id, name, colour: 0-7, x, y, z, yaw, pitch}`. `Step = {edits: [{x, y, z, before, after}]}` as `BlockStep` (`blockHistory.ts:31-34`), the values `state << 8 | fluid` or `0xFFFFFFFF` in the world's `stateTable` ids.
- `backupOf` is the original's id (the list shows its current name, so a rename shows). One normaliser in `saves.ts` reads every `state` and `history` record, at open and at import: a non-finite or out-of-range number (a pose field, `speed` outside 1-96, `t` outside [0, 1), `speedIndex` not an index of `TIME_SPEEDS`) takes its default, a `mode` other than 'walk' or 'fly' is 'fly', the hotbar goes through `normalizeHotbar` (`src/player/hotbar.ts:84-92`), places past 256 or with a colour outside 0-7 are dropped and names are cut to 64 characters, −0 becomes 0 (`canonicalJSON` throws on it, `src/core/params/canonical.ts:52-53`), and a history step with any edit that `editOk` would refuse after the remap (`src/workers/protocol.ts:661-666`) is dropped, as are steps past 100 per side. `persist/` value-imports the L2 core only (`test/arch/rules/imports.ts:11`, `:24`), so `saves.ts` cannot import `normalizeHotbar`, `SPEED_MIN`/`SPEED_MAX` (`src/player/fly.ts:32-33`), `TIME_SPEEDS`, the camera's `Y_MIN`/`Y_MAX` (`src/ui/world/query.ts:29-30`) or `editOk`: the normaliser takes those bounds injected (`StateBounds`, which the UI builds from them and a unit test pins equal), and checks a history edit with core's `WINDOW_HALF` and `yInWorld` (`src/core/coords.ts:23`, `:36`) and `REGISTRY.stateCount`, the bounds `editOk` reads. A saved pose's and a place's x and z are bounded by `WINDOW_HALF`, not by `?at`'s `AT_LIMIT` (500,000, `src/ui/world/query.ts:27`): flight has no horizontal limit (`src/player/fly.ts:8-10`), so a player beyond ±500,000 still opens exactly where they left (D4). The tick's state snapshot is built through the same rules, so Export never meets −0 or a non-finite number.
- A colKey is below 2^32 (`src/core/coords.ts:3`, `:50`), so `[worldId, 0] … [worldId, 2^32)` is one world's key range.
- `persist/kv.ts`'s interface is narrow: `read(requests)`, several `get`, `getAll(store, range)` and `count(store, range)` in one readonly transaction, and `write(batch) → Promise<{status: 'complete' | 'abort' | 'closed', errorName: string | null}>`, one readwrite transaction per batch of puts and deletes, so atomicity is the adapter's and `saves.ts` never sees a request. There is no cursor. `errorName` carries `QuotaExceededError`; `closed` is a connection the browser closed (site data cleared: its `close` event), shown as D14's "storage was closed". `IdbKV` opens one connection per thread; on `versionchange` it closes itself and the page shows "Reload to continue"; on `blocked`, "Close other tabs of world-imaginer-voxel". `close()` ends a connection: the bench and the selftest close every connection to their database (the page's and the sim's) before `deleteDatabase`, which otherwise waits on `blocked`. The durability option is left at the browser's default.

### 2.2 `ColumnDiffV1` (D21; master §2.7)

Header `u8 v` = 1, `u32 genKeyLo`, `u32 genKeyHi`, `u8 sections` (D21); per section `u8 sy, u8 fmt, u16 n`; fmt 0 sparse, n × `{u16 idx, u16 state, u8 fluid}` (5 B); fmt 1 dense when n ≥ 2560: 512 B mask, 8192 B states, 4096 B fluid; trailer `u16 count` (0 until SP7) and `{u32 packedPos, u16 dueOffset}` ticks; sections by sy, entries by idx, ticks by packedPos; little-endian. Its input is `columnDiff(cx, cz)`'s canonical arrays (`src/sim/diffStore.ts:60-79`). `encode` returns a buffer of exactly its size, never a view of a scratch buffer (IndexedDB and `postMessage` clone a view's whole `ArrayBuffer`). Decode rejects, with an error and never a partial diff: a bad `v` or `fmt`; more than 24 sections, `sy` ≥ 24 or sections out of order; a section with n = 0, a sparse one with n ≥ 2560, or a dense one whose mask's popcount is not n; `idx` ≥ 4096; unsorted or duplicate entries or ticks; a state ≥ the stored `stateTable`'s length; a fluid byte with its reserved bit 7 set (`src/world/blocks/fluid.ts:1-5`); a short or over-long buffer. An `idx` ≥ 4096 that reached the DiffStore would fail the sim at its column's first `diff` reply (`diffEntriesOk`, `src/workers/protocol.ts:684-687`, `:701-705`).

### 2.3 The remap (D22)

`saves.ts` builds the remap `stored id → today's id` from a stored `stateTable` (`parseStateKey`, `registry.ts:119-121`); a table equal to today's skips it, and a prefix of today's maps every id to itself. It runs in the sim on load before any value reaches the DiffStore (a state ≥ `stateCount` would fail every D of its column, `src/workers/protocol.ts:661`), on the history at open (on the main thread, before `load` is posted), and on import. When the stored table differs from today's, `load` carries `rewrite: {world, history}` (the record with today's table, and the remapped history when the stored table is not a prefix), and the sim writes, in one transaction before answering `loaded`, `worlds` and, when not a prefix, every column re-encoded under today's ids and the history (D22). From then on the stored table and the ids on disk agree.

### 2.4 `.wiworld` (D15, D23)

- A gzip `CompressionStream` over: `"WI10"`, `u32 version` = 1, `u32 headerLen`, the header as UTF-8 canonical JSON (`canonicalJSON`, `src/core/params/canonical.ts:94`), then one record per edited column, sorted by colKey: `i32 cx, i32 cz, u32 len, bytes` (the stored `ColumnDiffV1`, copied without decoding). The header: `{format: 'wi10-world', world: the worlds record without id (its `stateTable` describes the records' ids, D22), state: the state record without worldId, history}`.
- Export runs in the menu from the last committed save: one readonly transaction reads the world's three records and its `diffs` range (`read`, §2.1), so a world open in another tab is read at one `flushSeq`; the world is then held once in memory (as the sim holds it, D19) while the gzip stream is written; a `Blob` download helper joins `downloadText` (`src/ui/common/files.ts:10`).
- Import checks the magic, the version (newer is refused) and the header's `format`, loads the parameters as D33 says, normalises `state` and `history` (§2.1), checks each record's framing (a record past the end refuses the file) and bounds, decodes each record (one that fails is skipped and counted, as on open), recomputes genKey (an older one follows D8), remaps through the header's `stateTable`, re-encodes under today's ids, and writes a new id with today's `stateTable` in one transaction. Any refusal writes nothing.
- E2's hash is `fnv1a64` over the header's canonical JSON with `id`, `worldId` and `updated` removed, followed by every record in colKey order, decoded and re-encoded under today's ids.

## 3. Saving and loading

### 3.1 Who writes what

The worlds menu writes through `saves.ts` on the main thread when no world is open in that tab. During play the sim worker is the only writer of the open world: the main thread sends it the state (`flush`), a changed record (`record`, after an Apply or the older-generator open) and the copy request (`backup`). The menu's writes to a world open in another tab are refused by the lock (D7); Export only reads.

### 3.2 The flush (D5, D18)

- **Triggers** (`ui/world/autosave.ts`): a 2,000 ms timer, independent of frames (the frame pauses under overlays); `visibilitychange` to hidden; `pagehide`; `beforeunload`; and before every navigation the page starts, which waits for `flushed` up to 1,000 ms: D31's Worlds menu, and in a saved world the failure panel's Reload and the lost graphics context's Reload (`worldPage.ts:2245`, `:2276`), both retargeted to `/?world=<id>[&rd=<rd>]` by `here` (D4; `hereUrl`, `src/ui/world/reload.ts:42-46`, builds `/?at=…#<hash>` today, which would open an unsaved copy). The loading failure's lower-RD link (`worldPage.ts:1493-1496`, `:1529`) is retargeted the same way, with nothing to save before the reveal. There is no render-distance reload: the RD applies live (`rdControl`, `worldPage.ts:2398-2413`; the smoke checks "no navigation", `test/tools/uiSmoke.ts:2240-2241`). A failure after the reveal posts one flush and waits for `flushed` up to 1,000 ms before `failAfterReveal` releases the sim (`worldPage.ts:2239-2256`; `dispose` terminates the worker at once, `src/engine/simClient.ts:156-167`), unless the sim is what failed. A tick posts when something changed since the last committed flush: an `edited` reply, the pose, the time (not while `?time` fixes the clock: the clock is then not saved, `worldPage.ts:1741-1743`), the hotbar, the places, the history, a pending `record` or a failed flush. The timer's tick first waits for the edit path to settle (`settled()`, `src/ui/world/blockHistory.ts:57`), at most 1,000 ms, then builds the state and history in the same task, so a flushed edit's step is in the same flush; the hide and close triggers post at once. The state object is built at the tick, never per frame.
- **In the sim:** on `flush {state, history | null}`, if a flush is in flight the new one replaces any queued one (its state is newer; a queued history is kept unless replaced); otherwise: take the dirty-column set and clear it; for each, put `encode(columnDiff(cx, cz))` when the column holds entries, else delete its key; put `state` with `flushSeq` = last committed + 1 (the stored value arrives with `load`); put `history` if given; put a pending `record` if one arrived; all in one `write`. On `'complete'`: last committed = that `flushSeq`, reply `flushed {ok: true, flushSeq, columns, bytes, syncMs, commitMs}`. On `'abort'` or `'closed'`: merge the taken columns back into the dirty set, keep the history and the `record` as pending unless a newer one arrived, reply `flushed {ok: false, error}`; a `QuotaExceededError` shows D14's notice. Until a flush succeeds the main thread counts the state as changed, so every later tick and every hide or close trigger posts again.
- **The dirty set:** the colKeys written at `src/sim/edits.ts:375-376` and restored at `:397-398`. An edit between a flush's encode and its completion marks its column again, so the next flush carries it.
- **What a close loses:** at most the edits after the last completed flush, 2 s plus one commit, when the hide/close flush does not finish; whether a worker's transaction started at `pagehide` commits before the page is torn down is measured by the smoke's `save` step in Chrome and the user's check in Firefox, and the bound above never relies on it. The block history is posted with the state, and the timer's tick waits for the edit path to settle first; only a hide or close flush posted while a reply is outstanding saves an edit without its step: after a reload that edit is kept but cannot be undone, and an undo that meets it drops the stale step (D6, D32).
- A failed sim (the client terminates it at once, `src/engine/simClient.ts:80-90`) writes nothing more; the latched handler never flushes (`simHandler.ts:51-52`).

### 3.3 The protocol

`ToSim` (`protocol.ts:632`) gains `load {id, db, worldId, flushSeq, genKey, updated, stateTable, rewrite: {world, history} | null}` (§2.3; `genKey` the world record's 16 hex digits, which the sim writes into each flushed column's header until a `record` replaces it, D21, and `updated` the stored state's, which the backup's copy takes, §3.6: the sim reads only the `diffs` range, and `rewrite` is null for an ordinary world), `flush {id, state, history}` (`history` null, the record, or its change against the history the last `flush` carrying one resolved to: each side's run of kept steps and the steps added, which the sim resolves on arrival to the same record; D28's third lever), `record {world}` and `backup {id, newWorld, newId, state, history}`; `FromSim` (`:651`) gains `loaded {id, entryCols, entries, unknownStates, skippedColumns}`, `flushed`, `backedUp {id, ok, error}`; `edited` replaces `entryCols` with `entryAdded` and `entryRemoved` (D20). `parseToSim`/`parseFromSim` (`protocol.ts:667-713`) check each. The handler becomes `handle(m, post)` (D18): synchronous messages are answered at once as today; `load`, `flush` and `backup` post their reply when the transaction settles; until `load` has settled, `edits` and `diff` wait in arrival order. `sim.worker.ts`, `test/harness/inProcessSim.ts` (which posts `handle`'s result at once today, `:31-32`) and `test/harness/nodeWorker.ts`'s generated wrapper (`:83-84`, through which the integration test runs the sim, `test/integration/edit.test.ts:270`) pass `post`; for §6.3's `load` → `flush` → `load` round trip the wrapper gains a harness-only renew message that rebuilds the handler over the same in-worker `MemoryKV`.

## 4. Failure modes and review focus

- **A partial save** (some columns of a flush written, the state not): impossible inside one transaction; E4 injects an abort at every operation and a crash before completion.
- **Two writers of one world** (two tabs, or the menu and a tab): the lock (§3.5) and the menu's lock check (§1.3); the smoke's `two tabs` step.
- **A stored state id read as today's id:** the remap before the DiffStore (§2.3); E7's remap part.
- **Saved edits invisible until the first edit** (the entry set not seeded before the first D, `simClient.ts:152`): `loaded` awaited before the first `update` (§3.4); E3.
- **The flush delaying edit replies** (the stutter criterion): encode only dirty columns, a separate message so queued edits are answered first, the `persist.flush` gate and L11 (§6.2).
- **The edited-column set growing per click:** D20.
- **A terrain change escaping the mode:** every session mutator behind `setWritable` (D25); review that nothing changes the parameters except through the session (`mapTool.ts:142-148` and the direct calls the recon lists: gestures, presets, the profile confirm, the notice's Undo).
- **A backup missing edits from the last 2 s:** the copy runs after the pending flush, in the same transaction (§3.6); E6.
- **A world that cannot open** (a corrupt column, an unknown state, a newer format): skipped and counted, air with a count, refused with a reason (§2.3, §3.4, D14); never a load that stops.
- **A stored `stateTable` older than the ids on disk** (blocks of a later SP's states turning to air at the next open): D22's write at open; E7's sequence case.
- **A parameter leaf added by a later SP making every saved world unopenable:** D33 and its committed fixture.
- **A bad `state` or `history` record:** §2.1's normaliser; the world opens with defaults (D14).
- **A failure after the reveal killing a healthy sim unsaved:** §3.2's last flush before `failAfterReveal` releases it.
- **An undo stuck across reloads:** the tick's settled wait (§3.2) and D32.
- **A reload refused by the tab's own lock**, or a page kept in the back/forward cache holding it: D24's 2 s wait, the release on `pagehide`; the smoke's `two tabs` step.
- **The `pagehide` flush not committing:** bounded by the 2 s tick (§3.2), measured, not relied on.
- **Machine-state drift on the Iris Xe** (SP5a R52) moving the browser gates: the gates compare legs of one run (D28).
- **CI's wall growing:** §7.

## 5. What is kept

The generated world (no change to `gen/`, `GENERATOR_VERSION` 5, goldens and the schema-shape lock untouched); SP5a's edit path, read-through and history semantics; SP4c's map, teleport and Apply in unsaved worlds and, with the mode on, in saved ones; the map page `?map` (always editable, no Places tab).
