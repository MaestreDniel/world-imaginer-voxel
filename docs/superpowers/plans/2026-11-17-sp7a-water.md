# SP7a — Water in the generated world: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Water lives where the terrain puts it. On mountains a spring flows out of a rock face here and there and falls at least 8 blocks as a permanent waterfall, then spreads over about 7 blocks; floating islands pour long waterfalls from their edges; seas, rivers and lakes fill at their own levels and their old walls of water now flow down as short spills. Underground, caves under the sea and rivers are flooded through cracks in the sea floor, caves under dry land below sea level stay mostly dry behind rock barriers, some deep caves hold lakes of water or lava, the deepest gullies end in a lava puddle, and the abyss holds no water at all: it is lit only by lava falling from springs in its walls and pooling on its floor. Flowing water and lava show their slopes, falls and steps; at Medium water is Fancy (Fresnel and ripples, live Fast/Fancy toggle); under water the view closes into a blue fog, in lava into a short red one; walking through a thin film no longer swims; flooded caves are tinted in the X-ray and the slice shows aquifer water. Size XL (the user's Point 1 "Completo, ~52"; this plan has 58 tasks: Point 1's 52 plus the conditional lever tasks and the whole-branch review). About 50 visible waterfalls per km² of steep land (Point 2 "Voy a ir con unas 50/km2", gated 25-100), lakes sealed (Point 3 "Lagos sellados"), the gates in force (Point 4 "Límites vigentes"), and the user's addition at approval: "evitar al máximo posible los cortes abruptos de las cascadas, si hay algún caso lo tendré que ver dentro del mundo para saber si rompe mucho la experiencia inmersiva o no" (`A5.springsTruncated` pushed toward 0 lever by lever, Task 28, and one `truncatedN` review link per truncated spring near spawn for the user's check, Tasks 51 and 58). The generated world changes once: `GENERATOR_VERSION` 9 → 10 in the generator push (11 only after a retune the user's check asks for).
