# SP7b — The water is seen falling, and the surface is right: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The first time you come near a spring, its water runs out of the rock face and falls block by block at 4 blocks per second (lava in the abyss six times slower), spreads over the ground below and stops exactly where today's world has it; lakes, the sea, rivers, flooded caves and the water they spill are already full; a fall you have seen never falls again in the session, and reopening a saved world shows the falls around you complete. How near "near" is becomes a setting, the simulation distance (2 … your render distance, 6 by default); beyond it an unseen fall shows only its notch. Flooded caves fill to their rock ceilings, coplanar sloped water tops merge, Fancy's spill facets are softened, and a `riverMouth` review link exists. At the start the machine is re-checked and every baseline re-recorded after Ubuntu 26.04.1 under Wayland, and CI is brought back to 6 min 0 s. Size L (the user's Point 1, "Ok a todo": the full L, about 34 tasks, realistic ceiling 40; this plan has Point 1's 34 as 35 tasks (Point 1's "CI at the exit with the exit" split into Tasks 38 and 39), plus the two conditional lever tasks 8 and 30 and the whole-branch review 36: 38 core tasks, inside the ceiling of 40; and the optional stretch 33-35, D31, outside the count and droppable by the controller: 41 tasks in all). The generated world, the goldens and the saves do not change: `GENERATOR_VERSION` stays 10, the 68 golden keys keep their values, no save format moves.

## Review Focus

The spec implies these inputs, but no test it names exercises them. They are the likeliest ways a person would see a reveal go wrong, most likely first; each names the test that pins it and the task that owns it.

1. **A tab switched away mid-reveal, then back.** A 100-block island fall half revealed, the tab hidden for 30 s, then shown again. A person expects the fall to continue from where it was, not to jump to complete in one frame or write 400 quads at once. Task 16 pins in `test/unit/revealManager.test.ts`: "a set revealing at hop 200 of 400 (water): one step with `stepS` 0.25 after a 30 s gap writes only hop 201's quads (the clock advanced 0.25 s, Spec correction 6), and `writesThisFrame` equals hop 201's listed quad count (its shows plus the caps whose hide is hop 201)"; Task 16 also pins in `test/unit/frameStep.test.ts`: "a rendered tick 30,000 ms after the last rendered frame writes `state.stepS` = 0.25".
2. **A neighbour's late light or edit remesh swapping a column mid-reveal.** The column holding a fall's middle is replaced (SP4c's swap) while its spring is at hop 40, because its neighbour's L landed late. A person expects no flash of the complete fall and no hole: the replacement shows exactly what the old record showed. Tasks 17 and 18 pin in `test/unit/regionBatch.test.ts` and `test/unit/regionMesh.test.ts`: "a swap at clock hop 40: the new record's listed quads with show ≤ 40 < hide are drawn and the others degenerate, in the swap's own pump; max(index) < the vertex count after the swap and after an `optimize` (RegionBatch) or a compaction (RegionMesh) in the same frame".
3. **A world created in the worlds menu opening with its falls already complete.** The menu creates the record (`saves.create`, `src/ui/worldsMenu.ts:675`) and then navigates to `?world=<id>` (`:685`, a full page load), so on the world page a just-created world is a record that existed before the load: a rule "opened = a record that existed before this page load" calls `openDisc` and the spawn's falls never play, which is the first thing the user would try. The converse also bites: a reload of a saved world in the middle of a reveal must show the falls around the camera complete, not replay them. Task 19 pins in `test/unit/worldPage.test.ts`: "`revealOpenOf` is `'new'` for a record whose state has no saved pose (`player === null`, `newState`, `src/persist/saves.ts:316`: a world just created by the menu), and `'opened'` for a record with a saved pose (a reload, or a menu Play of a world flown in before)"; and in `revealManager.test.ts`: "a new world's spawn sets start at the first live frame; an opened world's sets staged before the lift are done at the lift".
4. **The simulation distance changed live while falls reveal.** At RD 16 the user raises Simulation distance from 6 to 16 (every unseen spring in the disc starts in one frame), then lowers it to 2 while those falls are halfway down. A person expects the raise to start them without a frame spike or a flash of complete falls, and the lowering to leave running falls running (D10: "reveals already running continue"), never freezing or re-hiding them. Task 16 pins in `revealManager.test.ts`: "40 unseen sets inside a 16-chunk disc, `simDistance` raised from 6 to 16: all 40 start in that frame and the step writes only their hop-0 quads (`writesThisFrame` equals that count, no hidden quad written); `simDistance` lowered to 2 while 10 sets reveal: their walks continue to done, and an unseen set at dx 4 does not start".
5. **An edit beside a revealing fall that spans a column outside the MESH disc.** A bucket placed next to a fall whose linked set has a member column at the MESH disc's edge, not yet meshed. A person expects the whole flow to appear at once where it is drawn, and the late column to mesh static (complete) when it arrives. Task 19 pins in `test/unit/revealManager.test.ts`: "`markEdited` on a column of the set: the set is done, its listed quads are written shown (caps hidden) at most 512 per frame, nearest columns first; a payload of a set member staged afterwards is written in its final state and its MESH request's `revealDone` has the member's bit set".

Also pinned elsewhere: flying away past RD+5 and back mid-reveal (Task 16's "freed and re-entered"; Task 12's freed-and-regenerated slot), and two meeting springs with one member outside the simulation disc (Task 16's "linked sets").
